## Product

ImpartialGames is a premium digital studio based in Montréal and Paris. We build custom websites, mobile apps, and SaaS products. 30+ clients. Tagline: "On construit ce que tes concurrents n'ont pas encore."

## Brand voice

Direct, confident, tutoiement. Short sentences. Zero marketing jargon. French-first, English secondary.

## Design direction

Light editorial premium. Gloock display serif + Manrope body + JetBrains Mono labels. Single accent: violet oklch(0.48 0.165 278) ≈ #6D5BD0. One dark section per page maximum (#0E0B14). Expansive negative space. Angular (4–8px radius). Editorial grid. Monospace details as dev identity signal.

## Pages

Homepage (7 sections: Hero, Projets, Services, Manifeste, Preuve, Equipe, CTA+Footer), Portfolio (listing + 4 project pages), Services (4 pages), Studio/About, Contact, Legal (4 pages). Admin/client dashboard is a separate auth zone.
